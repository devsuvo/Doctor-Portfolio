Hypertension & Heart Health

Protect your heart with early and consistent care.

Overview

High blood pressure often has no symptoms, yet it is a major risk factor for stroke, heart disease and kidney problems. Regular checks and proper control can prevent serious complications.

What We Treat

High Blood Pressure

Essential hypertension, isolated systolic pressure, and resistant hypertension.

Blood Pressure Fluctuations

Sudden spikes, white-coat hypertension, and unstable postural variations.

Cholesterol & Lipid Disorders

High LDL cholesterol, hypertriglyceridemia, and arterial plaque prevention.

Lifestyle-Related Heart Risk

Cardiovascular risk reduction for smokers, desk workers, and sedentary patients.

Warning Signs

Frequent headaches
Dizziness
Chest discomfort
Shortness of breath
Blurred vision

Our Approach

1

Accurate blood pressure assessment

Standardized readings taken after resting, validated against home monitoring records.

2

Cardiovascular risk stratification, lipid profiling, ECG, and kidney function analysis.

3

Medication tailored to your needs

Optimal anti-hypertensive regimen chosen for maximum protection of your kidneys and brain.

Salt, diet and exercise guidance

Practical sodium reduction techniques suited for Bangladeshi meals, DASH diet, and daily brisk walking.

5

Regular follow-up

Continual pressure monitoring, timely dose titrations, and ongoing reassurance to maintain optimal arterial protection.
