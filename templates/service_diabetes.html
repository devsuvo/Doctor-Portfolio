Diabetes Management

Take control of your blood sugar and protect your long-term health.

Overview

Diabetes is a lifelong condition, but with the right plan it can be managed very well. Dr. Tanvir creates a personalized plan based on your age, lifestyle, diet and health history.

What We Treat

Type 1 Diabetes

Intensive insulin management and ketone monitoring.

Type 2 Diabetes

Metabolic control, oral medications, and insulin titration.

Gestational Diabetes

Safe pregnancy sugar regulation protecting mother & baby.

Pre-diabetes

Lifestyle reversal strategies before full diabetes onset.

Diabetic Complications

Comprehensive kidney, nerve, eye and foot care guidance to avoid long-term organ damage.

Common Symptoms

Frequent urination
Excessive thirst and hunger
Unexplained weight loss
Blurred vision
Slow-healing wounds
Constant tiredness

Our Approach

1

Detailed Evaluation & Blood Tests

Accurate fasting glucose, post-meal readings, HbA1c, and organ markers to assess whole metabolic health.

2

Personalized Medication or Insulin Plan

Modern oral agents or micro-titrated insulin regimens carefully chosen to prevent dangerous hypoglycemia.

3

Practical Diet & Exercise Guidance

Culturally realistic meal strategies tailored to Bangladeshi foods, physical ability, and daily routine.

4

Regular Follow-up & Sugar Monitoring

Continuous support and dose adjustments to keep HbA1c strictly within target ranges.

When to See a Doctor

If you notice any of the symptoms above, or if your sugar levels are not under control despite medication.
