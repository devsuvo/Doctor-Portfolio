Thyroid Disorders

Balanced thyroid, balanced life.

Overview

The thyroid gland controls your metabolism, energy and mood. When it is underactive or overactive, it can affect your whole body. Early diagnosis and proper treatment bring most patients back to normal life.

What We Treat

Hypothyroidism

Underactive thyroid, Hashimoto's thyroiditis, and sluggish metabolism.

Hyperthyroidism

Overactive thyroid gland, Graves' disease, and elevated metabolism.

Thyroid Nodules and Goitre

Neck swellings, thyroid ultrasound correlation, and nodular monitoring.

Thyroid Problems During Pregnancy

Hormonal calibration safeguarding fetal cognitive and maternal health.

Common Symptoms

Unexplained weight gain or loss
Tiredness and weakness
Hair loss and dry skin
Feeling too cold or too hot
Irregular periods
Swelling in the neck

Clinical evaluation and thyroid profile testing

Practical nutritional guidance on iodine, selenium, calcium absorption, and healthy weight management.
