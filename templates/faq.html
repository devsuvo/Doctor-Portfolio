Patient Help Centre

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your visit.

Appointments

Q: Do I need an appointment?

A: Appointments are recommended to reduce waiting time, but walk-in patients are also seen as schedule permits.

Q: How do I book an appointment?

A: You can use the appointment form on this website or call the chamber during visiting hours.

Q: Can I reschedule or cancel my appointment?

A: Yes. Please inform the chamber at least a few hours in advance by phone.

First Visit

Q: What should I bring to my first visit?

A: Please bring previous prescriptions, test reports and a list of medicines you are currently taking.

Q: How long does a consultation take?

A: A typical consultation takes 15 to 20 minutes so that every concern is properly addressed.

Q: Should I come fasting?

A: Only if you have been asked to do a fasting blood test. Otherwise you may eat normally.

Treatment

Q: Can diabetes be cured?

A: Type 2 diabetes cannot usually be cured, but it can be controlled very well, and in some cases it can go into remission with lifestyle change and proper treatment.

Q: Will I need to take medicine for life?

A: It depends on the condition. Some problems need short-term treatment while others need long-term control. Your doctor will explain clearly.

Q: Do you recommend unnecessary tests?

A: No. Tests are advised only when clinically needed.

Online & Other

Q: Is online consultation available?

A: Yes, follow-up consultations are available by phone or video for existing patients.

Q: Are my medical records kept private?

A: Yes. Patient privacy and confidentiality are always respected.

Q: Is this website a substitute for medical advice?

A: No. The information on this website is for general awareness. Please consult a doctor for personal medical advice.
