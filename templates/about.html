Physician Profile

Meet Dr. Tanvir Ahmed Chowdhury

Dr. Tanvir Ahmed Chowdhury is a Consultant Physician and Endocrinologist based in Sylhet. He completed his MBBS from Sylhet MAG Osmani Medical College and went on to earn his FCPS in Medicine, followed by an MD in Endocrinology from BSMMU, Dhaka.

With more than a decade of clinical experience, he focuses on the long-term management of diabetes, thyroid disorders, hypertension and lifestyle-related diseases.

His approach is simple: listen carefully, explain clearly, and treat with evidence-based care. Every consultation is unhurried, personal and focused on lasting results.
